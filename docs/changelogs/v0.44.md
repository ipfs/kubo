### 🔦 Highlights

#### 🚇 `ipfs p2p forward` requires a peer ID in the target

`ipfs p2p forward` now rejects a target address that does not name a peer, such as `/ip4/1.2.3.4/tcp/4001` or `/dns4/example.com/tcp/4001` without a trailing `/p2p/<peer-id>`. Until now such a target created a forwarder that could never connect, and the next `ipfs p2p ls` or `ipfs p2p close` failed and left every later `ipfs p2p` command hanging until the daemon restarted.

#### 📦️ Dependency updates

- update `go-libp2p` to [v0.50.0](https://github.com/libp2p/go-libp2p/releases/tag/v0.50.0)
- update `go-ds-pebble` to [v0.5.13](https://github.com/ipfs/go-ds-pebble/releases/tag/v0.5.13)
